How to play

  • Drag with your left thumb to steer. Push to the edge of the stick to boost (boosting burns a little of your size).
  • Tap SLIME with your right thumb to drop toxic slime behind you. Any slug that slides through it is out, including you!
  • Eat orbs and coins to grow. If your head touches another slug's body, you splat.
  • On a computer: steer with the mouse, hold the mouse button or Space to boost, press E or right-click to drop slime, Esc or P to pause.

Common questions

  • I lost my coins or skins. Progress is saved on your device only. Deleting the app (or clearing your browser's site data on the web) removes it.
  • How do I play with friends? Tap FRIENDS, then CREATE ROOM and share the 6-letter code. Friends tap FRIENDS, type the code and tap JOIN. The person who made the room needs to keep the game open.
  • Why do I see ads? FAT SLUG is free and paid for by ads: one after each run, plus optional ads to revive or double your coins.
  • Can I turn off personalized ads? On iPhone, go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking and turn FAT SLUG off.
  • Game Center leaderboards don't open. Make sure you are signed in to Game Center in the Settings app, then tap RANKS again.
