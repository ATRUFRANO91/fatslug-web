Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

FATSLUG is a free game for iPhone and the web (fatslug.com), paid for by ads. This policy explains what information the game uses and why. FATSLUG has no accounts, no sign-up and no in-app purchases.

What stays on your device

  • Your progress: coins, best length, skins, hats, pets, upgrades, missions and settings, plus the name you type for your slug. This is saved only on your device (or in your browser's storage on the web). Deleting the app or clearing site data removes it.

Ads

  • On iPhone, ads are served through Unity LevelPlay and the ad networks it works with. On the web, ads are served by Google AdSense.
  • To show and measure ads, these services may collect your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking when iPhone asks), IP address, device and browser type, approximate location derived from your IP address, and how you interact with ads.
  • On iPhone you choose whether apps may track you. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. If you say no, you still get ads, but they are less personalized.
  • Learn more: Unity privacy policy · How Google uses information for ads.

Playing with friends

  • Friend rooms use Unity Gaming Services to connect players. The game signs in anonymously (no email or password) to get a random player ID, and shares your slug name, appearance and in-game movement with the other players in your room while you play.

Game Center (iPhone)

  • If you are signed in to Game Center, your best length and Slime Royale wins are sent to Apple's Game Center leaderboards under your Game Center name. Apple's privacy policy applies to Game Center.

Children

FATSLUG is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

  • Turn off ad tracking on iPhone as described above, or limit ad personalization in your browser or Google account on the web.
  • Delete the app or clear fatslug.com's site data to erase everything the game saved on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
